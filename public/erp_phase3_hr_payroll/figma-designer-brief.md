# Figma Designer Brief - Phase 3 HR & Payroll

Goal: Design the HR & Payroll module for the Construction ERP using the same visual language as Phase 1 and Phase 2.

Screens:
1. HR Dashboard
2. Employee Listing
3. Add/Edit Employee
4. Employee Details
5. Employee Documents / IQAMA
6. Shift Management
7. Attendance Management
8. Leave Management
9. Overtime Management
10. Salary Structures
11. Payroll Processing
12. Payroll Details
13. End of Service Benefits

Important:
- Employee form must be section-based.
- Attendance must show manual/mobile/offline source and geo-fence status.
- Documents must show valid, expiring soon, and expired.
- Payroll is foundation only, not final Saudi payroll calculation.
- EOSB calculation is manual/editable in this phase.

Accounting Posting

Approved equipment costs can create journal entries in Phase 4 accounting.

Fuel: Debit Fuel Expense, Credit Cash/Bank/AP. Maintenance: Debit Maintenance Expense, Credit Inventory/Parts/AP/Cash. Rental: Debit Equipment Rental Expense, Credit AP. Depreciation: Debit Depreciation Expense, Credit Accumulated Depreciation.

# Figma Designer Brief - Phase 7 Equipment & Vehicle Tracking

Design the Equipment & Vehicle Tracking module using the same Seera ERP admin style.

Screens:
1. Equipment Dashboard
2. Equipment & Vehicles Listing
3. Add/Edit Equipment or Vehicle
4. Equipment Details
5. Project / Site Assignments
6. GPS Tracking Overview
7. Maintenance Jobs
8. Fuel Logs
9. Equipment Documents
10. Operators / Drivers
11. Utilization & Downtime
12. Project Equipment Costing
13. Accounting Posting
14. Equipment Reports

UX Requirements:
- Keep sidebar grouped and collapsible.
- Show equipment status clearly: Active, Assigned, Maintenance, Idle, Retired.
- Show GPS status: Online, Offline, Inside Geofence, Outside Geofence.
- Show document expiry alerts.
- Maintenance due and fuel anomaly cards should be prominent.
- Forms should be grouped into sections.
- Project/site context must be visible on assignment, fuel, maintenance, and costing screens.

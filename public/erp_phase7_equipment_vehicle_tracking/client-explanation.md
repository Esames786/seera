# Client Explanation - Phase 7 Equipment & Vehicle Tracking

Phase 7 builds the equipment and vehicle tracking layer of the ERP.

This phase includes equipment and vehicle master, project/site assignment, operator and driver assignment, GPS tracking status, maintenance jobs, fuel logs, vehicle/equipment documents, utilization and downtime, project equipment costing, and accounting posting foundation.

Why this phase matters:
Construction companies use expensive machinery and vehicles. This phase helps management know where each asset is, who is operating it, whether it is active or idle, how much fuel it consumes, what maintenance is due, and how much equipment cost belongs to each project.

Example workflow:
1. Equipment is assigned to a project/site.
2. Operator/driver is assigned.
3. Fuel and maintenance are logged.
4. Usage cost is calculated for the project.
5. Approved cost is posted into accounting.

# Client Explanation - Phase 6 Inventory & Warehouse

Phase 6 builds the inventory and warehouse layer of the ERP.

This phase includes:
- Materials and item master
- Categories and units
- Warehouse stock
- Stock on hand
- Purchase requests
- Purchase orders
- Goods receipt notes
- Stock issues to project/site
- Stock transfers
- Stock adjustments
- Stock ledger
- Inventory reports
- Accounting posting foundation

Why this phase matters:
Construction companies need proper control over materials, stock movement, warehouse balance, site consumption, and project cost. This phase shows what material is available, what needs to be purchased, where stock is located, and how much material cost belongs to each project.

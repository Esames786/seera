# Figma Designer Brief - Phase 6 Inventory & Warehouse

Design the Inventory & Warehouse module using the same Seera ERP admin visual style.

Screens:
1. Inventory Dashboard
2. Materials / Items
3. Categories & Units
4. Warehouses
5. Stock On Hand
6. Purchase Requests
7. Purchase Orders
8. Goods Receipt Notes
9. Stock Issues
10. Stock Transfers
11. Stock Adjustments
12. Stock Ledger
13. Inventory Reports
14. Sidebar Grouping Improvement

Important UX:
- Show warehouse, project, and site context on inventory screens.
- Stock status should be clear: OK, Low Stock, Out of Stock, Reserved.
- Purchase and receiving screens should show approval/status badges.
- GRN, issue, transfer, and adjustment must show accounting posting status.
- The sidebar should be grouped/collapsible because modules are now too many.
